1. Introduction

Welcome to PlantSage AI (package: com.plantsage.ai). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and related services collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our Android application.

By downloading, installing, or using PlantSage AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

🔒 Our Core Privacy Commitment

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or photos. Plant photographs are analyzed solely to provide plant disease identification, botanical classifications, and herbal care remedies.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data necessary to deliver accurate plant pathology diagnoses and personalized garden management:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Information: When you register an account or log in via Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture (if provided by Google).
  • Saved Plants & Garden Data: Custom plant notes, disease remedies saved to your personal library, and timestamps of your plant health checks.
  • User Inquiries: Any feedback, bug reports, or support tickets you submit directly to us.

B. Information Automatically Collected

  • Device & System Data: Device model, operating system version (Android), unique device identifiers, language settings, and app performance logs to diagnose crashes.
  • Diagnostic & Usage Telemetry: Anonymized metrics on feature usage, model inference latency, and error logs to enhance app stability.

3. Camera & Photos (Core Functional Feature)

PlantSage AI is an AI-powered botanical identification tool. To fulfill its primary functionality, the app requests access to:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively in real-time when you choose to take a photo of a plant leaf or medicinal herb for identification.
  • Photo Library / Storage: Used only when you choose to import an existing plant photo from your device's gallery.

Processing Policy: Photos are processed using our on-device TensorFlow Lite neural network or sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our secure FastAPI inference server to return classification scores. We do not inspect personal or non-botanical photos from your gallery.

4. Location Information

The app requests location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) for the following specific in-app features:

  • "Save to Garden" Discovery Tagging: Saving the geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, and optional reverse-geocoded address) of where a medicinal or garden plant was spotted.
  • Environmental Context (Air Quality & Weather): Fetching localized ambient conditions to assess plant stress and outdoor growing recommendations.
📍 No Background Tracking

Location data is accessed only in the foreground while the feature is actively in use. We never track or record your location in the background when the app is closed.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for specific, lawful purposes:

  • To diagnose plant pathogens and provide organic, biological, and chemical treatment plans.
  • To identify medicinal plant species and display botanical profiles and traditional herbal formulations.
  • To synchronize your saved plants, scans, and notes across your devices.
  • To maintain user accounts, authenticate logins, and protect against fraudulent activity.
  • To monitor app health, resolve crashes, and optimize machine learning model accuracy.

6. Third-Party Services & SDKs

We work with trusted third-party service providers that adhere to strict data privacy requirements:

  • Google Play Services: Core application dependencies and security integrity. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In: Secure authentication handling via OAuth 2.0 without ever accessing your Google account password.
  • Google Maps API: Visualizing plant discovery markers on your personal garden map.
  • Google Gemini API: Generative AI assistance for detailed botanical advice, organic remedy formulations, and dosage guidelines.
  • Inference & Cloud Backend: Secure API backend for machine learning inference and encrypted database synchronization.

8. Data Retention & User Rights

We retain your personal data and saved plant library for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your data at all times.

Your Rights:

  • Access & Export: Request a copy of your stored personal data.
  • Correction: Update or correct your profile details anytime in-app.
  • Permanent Erasure: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data.
🔒 Account & Data Control

You can request complete account and data deletion at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our data privacy desk at support@plantsage.ai.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)

PlantSage AI does not target, market to, or knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to remove the data and terminate the account.

10. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information. All communications between the mobile application and backend services are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS protocols. Passwords and sensitive authentication tokens are hashed using secure cryptographic algorithms.
